
# CHCUHR – Project Documentation

## 1. Project Overview

**CHCUHR** is an HR consulting and execution service designed for companies that need practical help with people management and HR processes.

The project focuses on helping companies **build, improve, and operate effective HR systems** that support business growth.

CHCUHR is part of a broader ecosystem connected to **SUHR**, an HR education and community platform.

The relationship between the two brands is:

- **SUHR** → HR education and HR community
- **CHCUHR** → HR consulting and HR implementation for companies

While SUHR focuses on **developing HR professionals**, CHCUHR focuses on **helping companies implement HR practices in real business environments**.

The two brands complement each other.

---

# 2. Mission

The mission of CHCUHR is:

> To help companies build better workplaces by implementing practical, modern, and business-oriented HR.

Many companies struggle because they either:

- do not have an HR department
- have HR that is overwhelmed by administration
- lack structured HR processes
- need help scaling their people operations

CHCUHR helps these companies **design and implement HR systems that support both employees and business performance.**

---

# 3. Target Clients

CHCUHR primarily serves **small and medium-sized companies**.

Typical characteristics:

- 20–200 employees
- growing companies
- companies without a strong HR department
- companies that need HR structure

Typical clients include:

- startups
- scale-ups
- technology companies
- service companies
- family businesses
- companies transitioning from informal HR to structured HR

---

# 4. Main Problems We Solve

Companies often struggle with:

- chaotic recruitment processes
- lack of onboarding structure
- unclear roles and responsibilities
- missing HR strategy
- poor communication between management and employees
- inconsistent performance management
- HR being purely administrative instead of strategic

CHCUHR helps companies **create HR systems that support both employees and business goals.**

---

# 5. Services

CHCUHR provides three main service areas.

## 5.1 Interim HR

Temporary HR leadership or HR support when companies need experienced HR expertise but do not want to hire a full-time HR manager.

Typical activities:

- acting as interim HR manager
- supporting leadership teams
- building HR foundations
- stabilizing HR processes
- helping during periods of growth or transformation

## 5.2 HR Projects

Implementation of specific HR initiatives.

Examples:

- recruitment process design
- onboarding system
- performance management
- competency frameworks
- HR strategy
- employer branding foundations
- HR documentation and policies

The focus is always on **practical implementation**, not only recommendations.

## 5.3 HR Outsourcing

Support with ongoing HR activities.

Examples:

- HR process management
- HR administration support
- employee lifecycle processes
- support for managers
- HR process improvement

# 6. Approach

CHCUHR believes that HR should always support business goals.

The approach combines:

### Business Thinking
HR solutions must support company strategy and growth.

### Practical Implementation
We do not only recommend solutions — we help implement them.

### Simplicity
HR processes should be clear and easy to use.

### Modern HR Practices
We use modern HR tools, data-driven approaches, and AI where appropriate.
